All procurement activities are governed by the Public Procurement Act 2007 and the Rivers State Procurement Law 2019. Bids must be submitted through the official BuyRS Government Procurement Portal at procurement.riversstate.gov.ng. The ICT Department does not receive physical bids or respond to unsolicited proposals.
