Who Must Register

Mandatory ICT Project Registration

Under the Rivers State ICT Policy Framework 2024, all ICT projects above ₦5,000,000 in value initiated by any government MDA must be registered with the ICT Department before procurement commences. This ensures interoperability, prevents duplication, and maintains security standards.
