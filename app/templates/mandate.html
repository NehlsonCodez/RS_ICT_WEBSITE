Legal Authority

Statutory Mandate

The Rivers State ICT Department was established under the Rivers State Science, Technology and Innovation Law, 2012 (as amended 2021), which confers on the department the authority and responsibility to plan, implement, regulate, and coordinate all information and communication technology activities across the Rivers State Government.

The department operates in alignment with the National Digital Economy Policy & Strategy (NDEPS) 2020–2030, the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, and the Rivers State Digital Transformation Agenda 2024–2028 — a four-year roadmap approved by the State Executive Council.

Strategic Objectives 2024–2028

  1. Achieve 80% digital literacy rate among Rivers State adults by 2028
  2. Connect all 200 public secondary schools to broadband by Q4 2026
  3. Digitise 100% of government service delivery touchpoints by 2027
  4. Ensure full NDPR compliance across all 48 MDAs by 2025 ✅
  5. Train 50,000 residents in digital skills by end of 2028
  6. Establish Rivers State as top-3 tech investment destination in Nigeria
