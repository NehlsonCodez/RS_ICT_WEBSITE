Why Get Licensed

Become an Approved RS Government ICT Vendor

The ICT Vendor Licensing scheme ensures that all companies supplying ICT goods and services to Rivers State Government MDAs meet minimum standards of technical capacity, financial integrity, and legal compliance. A valid RS ICT Vendor Licence is required to bid on any government ICT contract.

Licences are valid for 2 years and renewable. There are three licence tiers based on company turnover and project capacity.
