Your Right to Know

Freedom of Information Act 2011

The Freedom of Information (FOI) Act 2011 grants every Nigerian citizen the right to request and receive information held by public institutions, including the Rivers State ICT Department. We are committed to responding to all valid FOI requests within 7 working days as required by law.

Before submitting a request, please check our Resources page and the Transparency section — much of our information is proactively published and immediately available.

Exemptions Under the FOI Act

Certain categories of information may be withheld, including classified security information, ongoing litigation, personal data of third parties, and deliberative process documents. We will always notify you if an exemption applies and explain the grounds.
