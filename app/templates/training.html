About the Centre

Established 2012 · Upgraded 2023

Located at Rivers State ICT Department, Aba Expressway, Port Harcourt, the Rivers State ICT Training Centre is the flagship public technology education facility of the Rivers State Government. The centre provides world-class digital skills training to residents from all 23 local government areas.

With a capacity of 400 students per cohort, the centre runs morning, afternoon, evening, and weekend sessions to accommodate working professionals, students, and full-time trainees.

The centre operates in partnership with NITDA, Microsoft Nigeria, and Google for Education — ensuring our curriculum meets international industry standards.

Certification

What You Earn at the End

Participants who complete a program and pass the final assessment receive a Rivers State Government Digital Skills Certificate, signed by the Director General and verifiable online via the RSG Digital Skills Database.

Advanced program graduates also receive preparation for globally recognised certifications including CompTIA Security+, AWS Cloud Practitioner, and Microsoft Azure Fundamentals — with the ICT Department subsidising exam vouchers for qualifying completers.
