Our Commitment to Transparency

The Rivers State ICT Department is committed to open government and proactive information disclosure in compliance with the Freedom of Information Act 2011 and the Rivers State Fiscal Responsibility Law. All budget, procurement, and performance data is published here as part of our Open Data initiative. For information not listed here, submit an FOI request via the FOI Desk.
