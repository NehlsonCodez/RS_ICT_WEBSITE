📍 ICT Training Centre, Aba Expressway
Training & Capacity Building

Hands-On Digital Skills for Every Rivers State Resident

Our state-of-the-art computer laboratories give trainees direct, supervised access to modern workstations and industry-standard software. Every program includes practical exercises, real-world projects, and supervised lab time — not just theory.

The Rivers State ICT Training Centre runs four daily sessions — morning, afternoon, evening, and weekend — so anyone can learn, regardless of work or school commitments.

See the Facility View Programs
Modern Facilities

A Campus Built for Focused Digital Learning

Beyond the computer labs, the ICT Department campus includes covered outdoor collaboration spaces, a "Learn · Create · Connect" innovation pavilion, study areas, and a digital resource centre — all designed to support a full learning experience.

24/7 power supply, high-speed fibre internet, and accessible facilities ensure uninterrupted training for all students, including persons with disabilities.
